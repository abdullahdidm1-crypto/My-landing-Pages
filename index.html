Medical-Grade Cleanliness

Hospital-Grade Tool Sanitization Between Every Delhi Home Visit

When inviting a groomer into your home, tool hygiene is non-negotiable. Delhi’s warm and humid climate creates an ideal breeding ground for microbial skin issues, canine papillomavirus, and fungal yeast. Unsterilized clippers carried between homes are the number one cause of cross-household ringworm and flea outbreaks.

At ThePetNest, our stylists follow a 3-tier sterilization process. Every blade, curved shear, and slicker brush is soaked in veterinary disinfectant, treated with antibacterial spray, and sealed into individually packed kits before ringing your doorbell.

Veterinary Safety Protocol Our groomers use only pH-balanced shampoos specifically formulated for canine and feline skin (pH 6.5–7.5). Human shampoos or baby soaps strip your pet's protective sebum layer, causing severe itchiness, flaking, and hot spots.

Pet parents with sensitive or allergic dogs can also choose our hypoallergenic oatmeal baths, specifically proven to soothe common allergic reactions triggered by Delhi's airborne dust and smog.

Local Climate Protection

Combating Delhi Monsoons, Extreme Heat, and Winter Smog

Delhi NCR experiences some of the most drastic temperature and environmental swings in the subcontinent: scorching 46°C dry summers in May, heavy 90% humidity during the July–September monsoon, and dense particulate winter smog from November through January.

Monsoon Tick & Flea Flare-ups: Damp soil in Delhi parks like Lodhi Garden, Sanjay Van, and Aravalli Biodiversity Park triggers severe tick blooms. If your dog gets wet during a walk, moisture trapped under a dense coat leads to acute moist dermatitis ("hot spots"). Our anti-tick treatments eliminate parasites while preserving coat oils.

Important Veterinary Note If your pet displays red pustules, weeping skin lesions, severe hair loss, or bloody ear discharge, please consult a licensed vet immediately. Grooming soothes hygiene, but active bacterial infections require veterinary antibiotic or antifungal therapy.

Need a professional medical checkup before grooming? You can easily schedule an in-home veterinarian visit in Delhi or connect for an online vet consultation through PetNest.
